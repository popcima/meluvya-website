## Verified factual notes

- Search: Apple lists dates, places, categories, named people, visible text and captions as inputs; media type can narrow results. Descriptive Apple Intelligence search requires availability/enabling. Info exposes caption and stored dates.

- Printing: AirPrint requires a compatible network printer; phone and printer share the network. Photos supports one or multiple images. Print Center in App Switcher provides job status/cancellation. Exact paper/layout capabilities depend on printer; preview and proof suggestions are editorial, not device test findings.

- USB export: Files > Browse > Locations accesses a compatible drive. Apple requires a single data partition and listed supported file systems. External drives may need independent power. Export Unmodified Original does not include edits; the save destination may default to iCloud Drive. Transfer samples and verification routines are editorial. No advice to erase storage or delete the source.

- Shared Library: up to five others; participants can add/edit/delete; contributed items move from Personal Library; organizer storage supplies quota. Camera has sharing choices. Shared Albums have contribution controls and can be public; do not count toward iCloud storage. Comparison does not make unsupported original-quality claims.
