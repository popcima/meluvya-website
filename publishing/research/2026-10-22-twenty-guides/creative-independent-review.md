| scan-printed-photos-with-phone | Approve. Physical-print handling, PhotoScan capture, boundary/glare review and linking a print's front/back context form a distinct job from digital editing and preserving files. This adds a physical-maintenance workflow absent from the night-blur guide. |
| remove-red-eye-from-photos | Approve. Adobe supports the flash-artifact explanation and Pupil Size/Darken Amount controls. Apple supports Mac Photos Auto/manual correction and its video/animal-eye limitations. Mac and Photoshop controls are explicitly scoped; the draft distinguishes pupil correction from a global color cast and makes no health diagnosis. Existing direct-flash guidance does not teach this correction. |
| fix-perspective-in-phone-photos | Approve. Adobe's mobile Geometry and Guided Upright documentation supports the mode distinction, reference guides and empty-border cropping. The draft preserves version/plan caveats, warns about changed proportions, and avoids treating correction as measurement. Architectural convergence is distinct from portrait composition/cropping. |
| intentional-motion-blur-photography | Approve. Nikon supports the panning/fixed-camera distinction, light-trail example, adaptable 1/30 or 1/20 second starting points and tracking before/after release. These values are not presented as universal, and phone computational-mode limitations are explicit. Selecting deliberate motion is a distinct intent from repairing accidental night blur. |
| selective-color-photo-effect | Approve. Adobe supports the Hue/Saturation adjustment-layer method, mask visibility behavior, color-range refinement and distinction from the named Selective Color adjustment. The draft adds useful object-versus-hue, holes/overlap and edge-review decisions beyond the existing monochrome and saturation guides. It does not claim these are Meluvya tools. |
