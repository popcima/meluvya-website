## Accessible primary evidence

- Apple 148868, “Shared Albums limits,” September 14, 2026; fetched November 16 (HTTP 200). New albums created in version 27 or later of iOS, iPadOS, visionOS, macOS and on the web store original formats/full resolution and charge the owner, including participant contributions. Temporary albums are exempt. Earlier-version albums are quota-exempt; stills reduce to 2048 long-edge pixels, panoramas up to 5400 width, video up to 720p. Feature availability varies by region.
- Apple 108916, “Upgrade your shared albums,” September 14, 2026; fetched November 16 (HTTP 200). Existing old albums remain unchanged unless upgraded. Newly added items after upgrade have original resolution. Items present before upgrade remain quota-exempt; new additions charge the owner. iPhone/iPad route: Photos > Collections > Shared Albums > chosen album > More > Upgrade Shared Album. Upgraded access uses iOS 27/iPadOS 27/macOS 27 or later, or iCloud.com. The document promises original resolution for new additions; it does not promise restored detail in previously downloaded reduced files.
- Apple 148676, “File types that you can use in Shared Albums,” September 14, 2026; fetched November 16 (HTTP 200). Original-format/full-resolution storage on supported version-27 software; older downloads may omit original information. Used to distinguish pixel detail from metadata and editing requirements.
