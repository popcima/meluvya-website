# Independent editorial review: Apple photo workflows

Reviewed October 23, 2026 by the editing-guides reviewer. Decision: **approve all ten for the parent's publication checks. No required factual or editorial fix identified.**

## Scope actually reviewed

- Read all ten complete objects in `apple-articles.json` and their records in `apple-research.json`.
- Compared the proposed intents with all 65 existing titles and inspected the nearest existing guides: Live Photo key frames and Long Exposure, cutouts, Google Photos Archive/Locked Folder, deleted-photo recovery, iCloud sync/backup, AirDrop original data, location removal, self-timer portraits and perspective correction.
- Opened every cited Apple URL. Checked the consequential control paths and limitations in the relevant sections of the versioned iOS 26 guides, current support articles and the explicitly historical WWDC20 transcript. The Live Text guide initially returned a tool error, then loaded successfully on one retry.
- Independently read the three Shared Albums sources, including the distinction based on album creation version; did not rely only on the parent's earlier verification.
- Programmatically confirmed ten new unique slugs, null publication/modification dates, exactly three existing related slugs each, ten distinct image files, and exact preservation of each donor's image, alt text, position, variants, credit and caption.
- Reviewed source-derived wording across article titles/descriptions, answers, body, FAQs, source notes and research. The 20 cited source URLs are not reused across these ten articles. Technical summaries remain limited; the rest is original proposed workflow and reader decision guidance. No source-derived passage set was identified as exceeding 200 words for one URL, and there are no long verbatim quotations. This is an editorial attribution review, not a mechanical claim that every word in an original example came from a source.

This review does not claim hands-on device testing, renderer/build validation, live URL checks, search volume evidence or ranking potential. Those were outside the assigned review.

Additional original screenshots would be optional future improvements, not a publication prerequisite.
